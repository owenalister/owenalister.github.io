Lotophage

Lotophage is a co-op action rogue-lite developed in unreal engine. It was developed with a team of 2 programmers and 2 artists. It featured procedural level generation, multiple ai enemy types, boss battles, power-ups and multiple playable characters with different playstyles. The combat in the game is powered by the "Gameplay Ability System", an unreal engine plugin that allows for the modular creation of abilities, stats, stat modifiers and gameplay effects. My main role on the project was the combat and pickups system. I implemented the characters attack abilies and designed a variety of powerups from simple modifier types such as: attack boost, increased movement speed, cool down reduction as well as more complex powerups such as passive abilities that granted effects like: life steal, a focus state where chaining attacks without taking damage increases attack power and resetting the doge cooldown upon a critical strike.

  • Technology: UE4, Blueprints, C++, Gameplay Ability System
  • Key Details: online co-op, procedural level generation, melee and ranged combat, powerups
